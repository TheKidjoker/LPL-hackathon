How to score (0 to 100):
- 70 or higher: likely scam, the withdrawal should be held. Use this when several strong signals combine (new payee added recently, full or near-full liquidation, senior client, first crypto transfer, a note that repeats a scam script such as "safe account", "bank security told me", "keep this secret", or someone else directing the client).
- 40 to 69: unusual but plausibly legitimate, worth a look.
- Under 40: consistent with the client's normal behavior or a clear legitimate purpose to a known payee. A large one-off payment to a long-known payee with a stated legitimate reason (for example a home purchase through a known title company) is usually low risk on its own.
